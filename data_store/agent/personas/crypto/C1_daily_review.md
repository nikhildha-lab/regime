# C1 Daily review (07:05 IST daily - after the 05:30 UTC close, outside quiet hours)

## Steps
1. Health / shock / halt. Daily close: BTC / ETH / SOL change, regime and size, DVOL and its 5-day change (tested warning).
2. Overnight: biggest move and when (session), funding and OI change (context), any liquidation spike or exchange news.
3. Open ideas by call_id: status (facts). 4. Today's plan: levels (5-day range, supports / resistances), 0-2 ideas with full
   leverage / liquidation / funding maths, registered (agent_log.py call --leverage --liq-price), horizon in hours or days.
